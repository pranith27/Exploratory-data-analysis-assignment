Part 1 — Tabular: NYC Airbnb 2019

48,895 listings → 48,825 modelling rows · Predict nightly price

Price is driven by where and what kind: Manhattan median $150 vs Bronx $65, entire home/apt $160 vs shared room $45. The 10,052 missing review values are structural (zero-review listings), so 0-fill is correct. KMeans k = 3 (silhouette 0.217). AutoViML Bagging_Regressor: log-RMSE 0.424, R² 0.602, $MAE 51.44.

Part 1 — Time series: household power

2,075,259 minute rows → 34,589 hourly rows · Predict next-hour power

Strong daily shape (20:00 peak 1.899 kW, 04:00 trough 0.444 kW), weekends heavier than weekdays, December nearly triple August. ADF statistic −3.666 (p = 0.0046). Daily-profile clusters k = 2. AutoViML Bagging_Regressor: RMSE 0.481 kW, R² 0.566; best baseline HistGradientBoosting 0.469 kW (naive persistence 0.593 kW).

Executed notebook (71 cells) · Report · Open in Colab

Time-based split (last 20% as test), leakage-guarded lag features, gap-aware imputation. AutoViML's own output files are in the folder.
